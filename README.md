# Deckspace: bugs and ideas

**Deckspace** is a free, turn-based space strategy game you play in your browser: steer your ship
with a hand of cards, launch missiles and nukes, fire the rail gun, and outlast your opponents. Play
hot-seat, against the AI, online with friends, or through the campaign.

**Play it:** https://d18x9yuuollrdm.cloudfront.net/

Deckspace is in **beta**: under active development, so rules, balance and features change, and saves
may be reset.

## What this repo is for

This is the game's public tracker. The game's code is private and is not here, so there is nothing to
fork and pull requests are closed automatically.

- **[Report a bug](../../issues/new?template=bug.yml)**: something broke, looked wrong, or didn't do
  what the game said it would.
- **[Suggest an idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml)**: a new card, a rule change, a campaign
  sector, anything that would make the game better.

Please search the [open issues](../../issues) first; if yours is there, add a 👍 or a comment instead
of a new one.

## What happens next

Every report and idea is read and labelled. Bugs are reproduced where possible; good ideas are
considered for the game's backlog. Not every idea will be taken up, and there is no timeline. When a
fix or feature ships, its issue is closed with the version it shipped in (shown on the game's start
screen).

## Please note

- **This is not a support or contact channel.** Issues are public; don't post personal information.
- **Ideas are a gift.** By posting one, you agree it may be used, changed or not used in Deckspace,
  with no payment or credit owed.
- Deckspace is provided as is, and you play it at your own risk; see the Terms in the game.
- Be kind: see the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
