# Code of conduct

This tracker is for making Deckspace better. To keep it useful and friendly:

- **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No insults, harassment, or hateful language.
- **Stay on topic.** Bugs and ideas for Deckspace only; no advertising or spam.
- **Keep it safe.** Don't post personal information, yours or anyone else's.
- **No exploits in public.** If you find a way to cheat or break online games, describe the effect,
  not a recipe others could copy.

Comments or issues that break these rules may be hidden, edited, locked or deleted, and their authors
blocked, without warning.
